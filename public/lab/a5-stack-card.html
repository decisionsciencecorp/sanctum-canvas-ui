Stack and Card

A model reply starts in a Stack (invisible flex layout — row or column, with a gap) or a Card (a titled box with optional sources). Stack has no chrome of its own; the panels below are Callouts so you can see the arrangement. The source under the card is a link.

Stack

Same three children twice: once in a row, once in a column. Narrow the window and the row wraps. Nothing in the stack is a control.
